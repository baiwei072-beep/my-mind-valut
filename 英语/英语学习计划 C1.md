# English Speaking Plan: B1-B2 to C1

> 来源：随笔/英语学习计划 C1.md

---

## Current Level

B1-B2

## Target

C1 speaking ability in 8-12 months of consistent practice.

---

## Existing Habit

### Morning Read-Aloud: 10-15 Minutes Daily

Keep this habit, but upgrade it.

Don't just read. Perform the text:

- vary the pace
- stress key words
- pause for effect
- imitate natural rhythm

Best sources:

- The Economist
- BBC News
- TED Talk transcripts

C1 lives more in argument and opinion than in narrative.

---

## Practice Blocks

### Block A: Shadowing

10 minutes, 4 times per week.

Method:

1. Play 5 seconds.
2. Pause.
3. Repeat exactly.
4. Match rhythm, linking, and reduction.

Sources:

- TED Talks
- Huberman Lab clips
- NPR

### Block B: Structured Monologue

10 minutes, 3 times per week.

Pick a prompt and speak for 2-3 minutes without stopping. Record yourself. Listen back once.

Do not script it. The discomfort is the point.

Prompts:

- Explain a decision you regret.
- Argue for or against remote work.
- Describe a complex problem you solved.

### Block C: Weekly Input Binge

30-45 minutes, once per week.

Watch one full interview or documentary without subtitles. Note 5 phrases you would not naturally use, then use them in your next monologue.

---

## Warning

Read-aloud alone will stall progress. It builds pronunciation and rhythm, but C1 requires spontaneous production under pressure.

**Structured monologue is non-negotiable.**
